What it shows

  • Flap and spine: the sheet seen along its length: a back panel, the hinge, and the flap that lies over the front, with a block for the pack it wraps.
  • Bill-pocket gusset: one side pleat of the large pocket seen across its width. The V closes flat when the pocket is empty.
  • Each fold turns through a fixed length of sheet, so a fold that opens gets a larger radius.

What the colours are

  • The closed-form strain on the outside of each fold, from the simulation's geometry, scaled with the fold angle (the pleat result: strain follows the angle).
  • Not a leather simulation. It does not include set, the stitching or the grain.

What would make it real

  • Closed thickness at the fold edge-on with a ruler, and how much of it the hinge actually wraps.
  • One gusset crease photographed edge-on, empty and loaded, with a ruler: its radius is assumed here and sets the tightest fold.
  • The sheet thickness, measured at several points: it sets every radius here.
  • The flat pattern, to say which fold is which.

Source: retailer or reseller text and product photographs in the Wallet-corpus- archive (item map04). The photographs are research documentation only and are not shown here.
